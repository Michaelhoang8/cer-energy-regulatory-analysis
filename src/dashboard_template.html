Read the measures

Utilization divides summed throughput by summed capacity on days with both a finite throughput and positive capacity. It is calculated for this reporting point only.

Throughput can exceed reported estimated capacity. Such observations remain in the data and are flagged for review; they are not labelled as regulatory breaches. Missing values are not replaced with zero.
